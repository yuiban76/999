# Game Dashboard — Information Triage Override

This page keeps the City Pulse visual system, but reduces simultaneous information.

## Priority order

1. Current location and available actions.
2. Cash, debt, career and physical condition.
3. Online players and direct interaction.
4. Cooperative tasks and commissions.
5. History, city memory and long-term progress.

## Progressive disclosure

- The personal panel shows only identity, money, urgent debt, career and three survival stats by default.
- Housing, owned businesses, talent and ability history live under one clearly labelled disclosure.
- The multiplayer panel uses three top-level tabs: Players, Tasks and Records.
- Open location hours and decorative index numbers stay hidden; closed state remains explicit.
- No game capability or numerical rule is removed by this presentation layer.

## Copy

- Prefer direct Traditional Chinese labels.
- Remove repeated English display labels when the Chinese heading already communicates the same meaning.
- Keep warnings and actionable explanations; shorten passive system descriptions.
